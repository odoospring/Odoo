Key Odoo Integration Features

  • Omnichannel Order Sync: Automatically receive orders from major EDI retailers and Shopify into your Odoo system.
  • Fulfillment & Shipment: Push shipment data and statuses from Odoo back through Spring to update your channels.
  • Inventory Management: Push real-time inventory levels from Odoo out to your integrated sales platforms.
  • Flexible Directional Flow: Configure whether transactions originate from Odoo and flow to Spring, or vice versa.
  • Product Catalog Sync: Keep your product details aligned across all platforms seamlessly.

  • Direct connection to hundreds of major retail trading partners.
